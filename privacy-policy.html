What this policy covers

This policy explains what information Financial Frontline collects when you visit financialfrontline.co.in, and how it's used. It applies to this website only, not to YouTube, Telegram, or any other third-party platform where you may also interact with Financial Frontline.

This website does not currently use tracking cookies or analytics scripts. The information we do collect is limited to:

YouTube content on this site

This page displays videos and video data pulled from YouTube using the YouTube Data API. When you click through to watch a video, you leave this site and are subject to YouTube and Google's own privacy policy and any cookies or tracking they use.

FF Knights membership

FF Knights membership, billing, and any personal information tied to that membership is handled entirely through YouTube Membership, governed by Google and YouTube's privacy policy. Financial Frontline does not separately store your payment information.

How information is used

Any information you send us directly is used only to respond to you. Technical hosting logs are used only for keeping the site secure and functioning correctly.

Third-party services

This site links out to YouTube and Telegram. Financial Frontline is not responsible for how those platforms collect or use your information once you leave this site.

Children's privacy

This website and its content are intended for adults making their own financial education and trading decisions. It is not directed at, and should not be used by, anyone under 18.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated as the website and its services evolve. The updated date at the top of this page will reflect the most recent revision.
